k3s

k3s v1.36.5+k3s1 runs as a binary in /var/usrlocal/bin. It binds to the tailnet IP only, uses flannel on tailscale0, and has traefik and servicelb disabled. The kit is in home-k3s/karnataka/:

  • install-host.sh installs k3s.
  • apply-stack.sh installs KubeVirt v1.9.1, CDI v1.66.1, corral-web, the AMD GPU device plugin and Lemonade.

See Home k3s and KubeStellar for the shared design and the firewalld rules.

Hive standby

/var/srv/hives is a copy of the hive data. When bihar is active, bihar pushes it here every 15 min. The containers stay stopped until the lease on spindle names karnataka. See Hives hosting and failover.

Notes

  • Disk: /var is 88% full. The ARC runner PVCs and the macOS VM disks use most of the space. Check this before you add VMs.
  • punjab is a VM on this host. If karnataka restarts, punjab also restarts.
